Charger simulator

A stand-in charger for testing the CCMS app and backend, in OCPP 1.6J or 2.0.1. No hardware needed.

Chargers

Connect a charger

It must already exist in the backend (admin API or charger list).

Up to /ocpp. The charger ID is added for you.

Hardware and timing

Speed 60 delivers an hour of energy per minute. Timestamps stay real, so a charger with a max power set will flag the session for review.
